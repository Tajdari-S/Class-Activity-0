About

Research framing

I am a Systems Engineering PhD student focused on Human Factors research that examines the ways software developers interact with AI-driven development environments. After completing my BS in Computer Science at Virginia Tech in 2021 with a focus in Human-Computer Interaction, I now combine socio-technical research questions with hands-on embedded development to prototype supportive tooling for development teams.

Highlighted Projects

Selected work across academic research and prototyping

Bike Broadcast

Undergraduate capstone project delivering a wireless communication device for cyclists powered by a Raspberry Pi Zero and open-source software, enabling safer group rides through reliable messaging.

Pavlov's Desk

Led the development of a cyber-physical smart desk hutch prototype with multi-color lighting, scent diffusion, and airflow modules controlled by a Seeeduino Xiao to study adaptive workspaces.

Tactile Psychophysics Study

Designed and executed a research study investigating how people interpret haptic feedback, developing control software that orchestrates embedded actuators for lab collaborators.

BLE Heart-Rate Integration

Ongoing exploration into capturing Bluetooth Low Energy heart-rate data on the ESP32 using real-time techniques to support responsive human-in-the-loop prototypes.
